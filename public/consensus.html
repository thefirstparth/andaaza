How this page picks
  1. Read. The four hundred busiest questions on Polymarket, every open event on Kalshi, and Manifold's busiest play-money questions. Refreshed every five minutes, and the page updates itself while it is open.
  2. Clear the noise. Questions that are all but settled (above 98.5% or below 1.5%), markets with under $2,000 traded in a day, side bets (tosses, sets, handicaps) and price ladders go. So do your standing exclusions: US domestic politics, the Fed, American football, baseball, hockey and college sport, weather, and post or view counts.
  3. Sort. Each question goes to the first of your subjects whose words it contains: Formula 1, football, cricket, tennis, NBA, AI and tech, money, India, world, Bollywood and film, series and streaming, celebrities. A single match only shows when it involves someone you follow, or it is a very big football match.
  4. Merge. The same question on two sites becomes one card, the busier site leading and the other shown beside it.
  5. Rank. Within a subject, anything about the Warriors, Real Madrid, Verstappen, Alcaraz, Djokovic or India comes first, then the rest by money traded today. Play money appears at most twice per subject. Moving today is the real-money questions in your subjects that moved four points or more in a day. Miscellaneous is the busiest of everything that fits nowhere else.

A price is what traders pay for "yes", read as the chance they give it. Polymarket and Kalshi are real money; Manifold is play money and says so.
